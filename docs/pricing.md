## Questions people ask

**Why is Founder cheaper than Pro?**
Founder is our thank-you to early believers. It's the same report tier as Pro's base — Pro adds the harness (continuous watching + MCP + phone). Founder locks at $9; Pro is $19.

**What counts as a "loop" for the loop fee?**
One full scan of one repo = one loop. Re-scanning the same unchanged repo doesn't charge again — we dedupe.

**Do promo codes stack with Founder?**
Promo codes give free full loops (usually 3). They work on any account, including Free. They don't change your subscription.

**Can I get a discount?**
Founder at $9 locked forever *is* the discount. We don't do coupons, student plans, or one-off deals — one fair price, locked for early members.
