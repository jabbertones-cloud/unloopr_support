# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability
Do not open a public issue. Email the security contact in your Unloopr account settings with:
- What you found (no exploit code needed)
- Where (URL, tool, version)
- Impact as you understand it

We respond within 2 business days.

## Scope
Unloopr's website, MCP server, apps, and this support repo. Out of scope: third-party AI tools' behavior (report those to the tool vendor).

## Safe harbor
Good-faith research is welcome. Don't access other users' data, don't disrupt the service, don't exfiltrate.
